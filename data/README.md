# Data layout

Place occupancy-grid maps in `data/maps/`.

The camera-ready manuscript reports the following map resolutions and object representations:

| Environment | Resolution | 30 cm object footprint | Inflation radius |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Maze | 0.03 m/cell | 10 cells | 6 cells |
| Hospital A | 0.05 m/cell | 6 cells | 6 cells |
| Hospital B | 0.05 m/cell | 6 cells | 6 cells |
| Warehouse | 0.03 m/cell | 10 cells | 6 cells |
| Depot | 0.04 m/cell | 8 cells | 6 cells |

The original occupancy-grid files are not reconstructable from the manuscript alone. Add the original maps here before final archival.

Image convention used by the offline code: dark pixels are occupied and bright pixels are free.
